Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

PSYCHE is a personal application used by one person, its owner, on their own Mac and Android phone. This policy describes what it does with data from the owner's Google account.

What PSYCHE accesses

  • Google Sheets (spreadsheets scope): the spreadsheets the owner points PSYCHE at, such as their weight log, and the "PSYCHE inbox" spreadsheet it creates for entries made on the phone. PSYCHE reads the entries and writes the owner's corrections back.
  • Google Drive (drive.file scope): only files and folders PSYCHE created or the owner opened with it, such as the "PSYCHE inbox" folders where the phone puts photos of receipts and bills and PDFs of electricity and other bills. PSYCHE cannot see the rest of the owner's Drive.

On the phone

The Android app signs in through Google Play services with the same two permissions. It keeps what the owner enters, scanned receipts and bills shared to it from other apps (such as a PDF from the mail) on the phone only until they are sent to the owner's own Google Sheet or Drive folders, then removes them. Documents are scanned with Google's on-device scanner. The app has no server, stores no Google tokens, and sends nothing to the developer or anyone else.

Where the data goes

PSYCHE copies what it reads into files on the owner's Mac, protected by the Mac's disk encryption. It has no servers; nothing is sent to the developer or shared with anyone else. Google sign-in tokens are kept in the Mac's Keychain.

Only when the owner turns it on for a kind of document, PSYCHE sends that document (a receipt photo, a bill, a building notice) to an AI provider (Anthropic) to read its contents, either through an API key or through the owner's own signed-in Claude command, under Anthropic's terms for that use. Nothing is sent before the owner allows it, and the owner can withdraw that permission at any time.

Use of Google user data

PSYCHE's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to provide PSYCHE's features to its owner; it is not used for advertising, not sold, and not read by people other than the owner.

Keeping and deleting

The data stays on the owner's Mac until the owner deletes it. The owner can export it, delete PSYCHE's data folder, and remove PSYCHE's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions go to the owner, through the project's repository.
